What is it?

A free, virtual space to breathe, share, and celebrate the power of breath together.

No experience is needed.

Just you and your breath.

Soma+IQ™

Soma+IQ™ is a somatic breathwork practice that uses conscious breathing to help us reconnect with the body, release what we are holding, and return to a more present state.

How it works

We will guide you through breathwork practices that support clarity, connection, and vitality, followed by time to connect and share.

The experience is designed to be accessible whether this is your first breathwork session or breathwork is already part of your life.

Who is it for?

Everyone. Whether you're breathwork-curious or a seasoned breather, you are welcome.

Participant Acknowledgment & Release

By participating in Prana Party, I understand that breathwork is a voluntary wellness practice that may create physical, mental, or emotional effects, including changes in breathing, heart rate, body sensations, dizziness, tingling, emotional release, or temporary discomfort.

I understand that certain breathwork practices may not be appropriate for everyone. I should not participate in intensive breathwork without first consulting a qualified healthcare professional if I am pregnant, or if I have a history of cardiovascular disease, uncontrolled high blood pressure, epilepsy or seizures, serious respiratory conditions, glaucoma or retinal conditions, recent major surgery or physical injury, severe osteoporosis, or another medical condition that could be affected by changes in breathing or physical exertion.

If I have a serious medical or mental health condition, take medication that may affect my ability to safely participate, or have any uncertainty about whether breathwork is appropriate for me, I understand that I should consult my healthcare provider before participating.

I agree to listen to my body, participate within my own limits, and stop immediately if I feel unwell, distressed, dizzy, faint, or otherwise uncomfortable.

I understand that Prana Party and its facilitators do not provide medical, psychological, psychiatric, or therapeutic treatment, and participation is not a substitute for professional healthcare.
