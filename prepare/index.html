Prepare for Prana Party

A few simple things to know before we breathe together.

  • Friday, September 18, 2026
  • 5:30 PM to 7:30 PM EDT

Find a comfortable space. Bring your headphones. And come as you are.

What you'll need

  • A Comfortable Place to Lie Down

    Have a mat, yoga mat, or another comfortable surface where you can lie on your back.

  • Headphones or Earbuds

    Headphones or earbuds will help you stay immersed in the experience and hear the guidance clearly.

  • Zoom

    Make sure you have access to Zoom and that your camera and audio are working before we begin.

  • A Quiet, Private Space

    Choose somewhere you can breathe comfortably without being interrupted.

  • Set Up Your Camera

    When we begin the breathwork portion, you'll be lying on your back. Position your camera so that your upper body, including your chest and belly, can be seen comfortably. This allows the facilitator to observe your breathing and somatic response and guide the session live.

    Please turn off Zoom background blur, virtual backgrounds, and other camera background effects before we begin.

Give your body some space

Please don't eat anything for 1 hour before the session begins.

A little space in the body helps the breath move freely and keeps you comfortable throughout the practice.

What to expect

  1. Arrive

    Introduction

    We'll begin together, settle into the space, and introduce the Soma+IQ™ method and the experience ahead.

  2. Breathe

    Guided Breathwork

    You'll lie comfortably on your back while being guided through the Soma+IQ™ breathwork experience.

  3. Integrate

    Integration & Sharing

    We'll slow things down, come back together, integrate the experience, and leave space for sharing.

A few minutes before

  • Find your quiet space
  • Have your mat or comfortable surface ready
  • Put your headphones or earbuds nearby
  • Open Zoom and check your camera and audio
  • Turn off background blur and virtual backgrounds
  • Position your device so your upper body can be seen while lying down
  • Give yourself a few minutes to arrive without rushing

Come as you are.

Listen to your body

Breathwork can create strong physical and emotional experiences. If you are pregnant or have a medical condition that could be affected by intensive breathwork, please consult a qualified healthcare professional before participating.
